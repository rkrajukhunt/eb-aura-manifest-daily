# 15 — MONETIZATION & PAYWALL

## The contradiction (flagged per instructions)

- **RESEARCH FINDING (PDFs):** Annual hero $29.99–39.99/yr; Lifestyle free trials reduce LTV −21.2% (the only category where this happens); hard paywalls convert ~5x at D35; Health & Fitness categorization flips trial economics positive; pre-selected annual drives 60–70% to annual; localized pricing = highest-uplift experiment (+62.3% LTV).
- **COMPETITOR REALITY (extraction + Aura plan):** Stella $6.99/wk + 7-day trial; Aya $9.99/wk no trial. Both monetize successfully at weekly. Aura plan chose $6.99/wk + trial + $49.99 annual.
- **USER SENTIMENT (Stella reviews screenshot):** weekly pricing reads as predatory when discovered late ("$10/week!!! Greedy & misleading", "$40 a month US #… Be honest!"); trial promises that charge instantly destroy trust.

## PRODUCT DECISION (synthesis)

**Dual-SKU paywall, annual pre-selected, weekly available, radical transparency.**

- **Annual $39.99/yr** — hero, pre-selected, shown as "$39.99/year · about $3.30/month."
- **Weekly $6.99/wk** — for commitment-shy users, shown honestly as "$6.99/week · about $27/month" (the monthly equivalent is _printed_, not hidden — our trust signature; Stella's reviews prove hiding it costs stars).
- **Trial:** launch WITHOUT a free trial on annual (direct purchase on intent, per Lifestyle data) and WITH 7-day trial on weekly (matches category expectation). **EXPERIMENT #1:** trial vs no-trial cohorts via RevenueCat before scaling — the research explicitly warns not to assume.
- **Category:** Health & Fitness (defensible for affirmations/wellness; better trial economics + "I Am" precedent).
- **Lifetime (~$99.99):** V1.1 candidate for the anti-subscription cohort (a real complaint segment). LATER: localized pricing (highest-uplift lever).

## Free tier (hard-soft hybrid)

Full-screen paywall after the Letter, dismissible once → free tier: 1 daily moment + 1 daily affirmation + gratitude + the kept Letter. Locked: Manifest Anything, refine, favorites beyond the Letter, share-export, collections. The free tier is a daily reminder of the magic (Finch lesson: a great free experience converts better than a locked one) — and our review-score insurance.

## Paywall spec (first presentation, post-Letter)

- Inherits the Letter's visual world (same-world crossfade — see 13). Headline mirrors the wow: **"Your future self has more to tell you."**
- Contrast block (Aya's strongest pattern, honest version): _Today_ — "One letter. One morning." / _Every day_ — "A new moment each morning. Your memory deepening. Letters on day 7, 30, 100."
- Plan cards: annual pre-selected; weekly below with true monthly equivalent; ALL terms visible (renewal, cancel-anytime, trial terms restated on the native sheet).
- Social proof once real (rating + count). No fake urgency, no "team letters," no countdown timers, no discount-pressure second paywall (Aya's "quieter price" pattern is banned).
- Footer: Restore · Terms · Privacy. Dismiss (X, top corner, visible after 2s) → free tier + one quiet line: "The letter is yours either way."

## Anti-resentment checklist (release-blocking)

1. Price appears on the FIRST screen of the app ("Free to begin. Premium from $39.99/yr — you'll see everything before you decide") — effort is never invested blind.
2. Weekly price always shows monthly equivalent.
3. Trial terms restated at the moment of confirmation; reminder notification day 5 of any trial ("Your trial converts in 2 days — keep or cancel, both fine").
4. No paywall within or immediately after a vulnerable disclosure surface.
5. Manage/cancel reachable in 2 taps from Settings; no retention mazes.
6. Lapsed users keep their Letter and their data (win-back = warmth, not hostage).
7. No ads, ever, for anyone (a top "I Am" complaint).

## Second surfaces

Locked-feature sheets (calm, in-voice, honest price, no full-screen interrupt) · D7 milestone gentle annual upsell for weekly subscribers ("Same mornings, a third of the price") · lapse +3d win-back note (06).

## Unit economics guardrails (RESEARCH FINDING)

Every moment costs LLM+TTS; caching favorites, one-a-day pacing, and on-demand weekly limits are the cost model. Benchmarks to beat: D30 install→paid ~1.7% (upper quartile 4.2%); target install→paid ≥3–5%; wow→trial >35%; trial→paid >40%. RevenueCat (or equivalent) instrumented from day one for cohort truth.
