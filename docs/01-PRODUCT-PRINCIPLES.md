# 01 — PRODUCT PRINCIPLES

These are decision rules. When a feature debate happens, these win.

## 1. Connection over content

Every screen should make the user feel _seen_, not _served_. Copy uses their name, their words, their people. A smaller library that knows you beats a bigger library that doesn't.
**Test:** Could this screen exist unchanged for a different user? If yes, it's not done.

## 2. One wow, early — before the paywall

The emotional peak (the Future-Self Letter) happens in the first ~5 minutes, _before_ any payment ask. The wow does the selling. (PRODUCT DECISION — this reverses Stella's after-paywall model and directly answers its worst reviews.)

## 3. Radical pricing honesty

Price and trial terms are visible _before_ the user invests effort in onboarding, and again clearly at the paywall. No urgency theater ("overwhelming demand"), no surprise charges, no fake discounts. Stella's 1-star reviews ("bait and switch," "$49 charged as soon as I downloaded") are the cautionary tale. Trust is a feature; resentment is churn.

## 4. One habit a day

The daily ask is tiny and complete: listen to one moment, read one affirmation, write one gratitude line. Nothing nags for more. Countdown to tomorrow = anticipation, not FOMO.

## 5. Memory is used to delight, never to guilt

Callbacks celebrate ("Three weeks ago you said you were scared to start — listen to how far the story has come"). Missed days are never mentioned negatively; the companion is simply glad you're back. No broken-streak shame, ever.

## 6. Earn the subscription

The paywall converts because the wow proved the magic, not because features are held hostage mid-vulnerability. A small-but-real free tier (1 moment + 1 affirmation + gratitude daily) is a daily reminder of the magic, not a crippled product.

## 7. The user's words are sacred

Generated content reuses the user's exact phrases ("the cottage," "my own studio," "Mom") verbatim wherever natural. Never paraphrase their dream into template language.

## 8. Grounded magic, not wishful thinking

Moments describe the user _acting and arriving_, not passively wishing (answers the Oettingen critique that pure positive fantasy saps motivation). Affirmations follow evidence-based phrasing: present tense, positive framing, identity statements, tied to the user's stated values (Steele's self-affirmation theory). Techniques are taught honestly — as mental rehearsal and identity practice, not guaranteed cosmic outcomes.

## 9. Calm, premium, iOS-native

Apple design values: clarity, hierarchy, depth, fluid motion, generous whitespace, subtle haptics. 60fps or it doesn't ship. No glassmorphism-everywhere, no confetti on vulnerable moments, no gradient soup.

## 10. No dark patterns — explicit prohibitions

- No guilt notifications ("Your future self is waiting… 😢" is banned).
- No paywall inside or immediately after a vulnerable disclosure.
- No fake scarcity, fake countdowns, or fake "team notes."
- No hidden weekly price behind an annual-looking figure.
- No punishment mechanics for missed days.
- Cancel/manage subscription reachable in two taps from Settings.

## 11. Cost is a design constraint

Every generation costs LLM + TTS money. On-demand limits, caching favorites, and one-a-day pacing are cost features _and_ scarcity-as-specialness features. Design them together.

## 12. Ship the moat first

Stella/Aya can copy features; they cannot copy an accumulated memory. The Living Memory system (10) is V1-core, not a Phase-2 nice-to-have.
