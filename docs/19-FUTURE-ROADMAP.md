# 19 — FUTURE ROADMAP

Staging logic: V1 proves the wow converts and the ritual holds · V1.1 deepens connection with near-zero new surface area · V2 broadens the ritual and ships the costly natives · V3+ expands market. Nothing enters a phase without the prior phase's metrics (06) holding.

## V1 — Launch (see 05 for full list)

Conversational onboarding · Future-Self Letter · daily moments + player + refine · Manifest Anything (limited) · Affirmation Studio (daily + guided generation + inline techniques) · minimal gratitude · Living Memory v1 + What Aura Knows + Never-Include · D7 milestone letter + word echoes · notifications · dual-SKU transparent paywall + free tier · settings/deletion · analytics.
**Goal:** wow→trial >35%; D7 paid 60%; validate $39.99/yr hero.

## V1.1 — Deepen (weeks post-launch)

- Automated callback engine (3-week callbacks, anniversary echoes) + D30/D100 milestone letters.
- **Learn library** (full technique cards: 369, scripting, visualization, phrasing science, gratitude research) with practice links into the Studio.
- Custom affirmations + future-self rewrite.
- Data export · lifetime SKU test · localized pricing (highest-uplift lever) · paywall/trial A/B conclusions applied · second voice.
  **Goal:** lift D30 to 35%+; prove callback→favorite/retention lift (the moat metric).

## V2 — Broaden the ritual

- **Own-voice affirmations** (record → playback; consent flow per 18) — Innertune-validated, inherently screen-recordable growth content.
- **Home/lock-screen widget** (native WidgetKit; Aya-proven surface) — all-day reinforcement.
- **Vision Board with audio** — pin a moment to a board item ("hear this dream"); mark-as-manifested → celebration moment. No competitor links board→audio.
- Gratitude weaving fully automated (entries surface inside future moments).
- Ambient soundscapes (simplified Stella mixer) · read-aloud practice (speech recognition) · goal threads/tracks (money, career, love — ASO gold).
  **Goal:** D30 40%+; shares/WAU >10%; widget adoption as retention multiplier.

## V3+ — Expand (contingent on PMF)

- Android (only after iOS economics proven) · localization (JP, Nordics).
- Evidence-based "grounded mode" toggle (skeptic TAM) · faith-based sister mode (separate brand — research: underserved, high-trust).
- Community/success-story feed (moderated; UGC/testimonial engine) · referral loop (share → premium days).
- Companion chat (the explicitly-deferred feature — only if unit economics + safety design mature).
- Apple Watch · creator partnerships · web-to-app funnel.

## Growth roadmap (parallel to product — from research GTM)

Pre-launch: TikTok account live, founder-led + card content daily · Launch: ASO (keyword-first title "Aura: Manifest & Affirmations"-style, OCR-indexed screenshot captions, rating prompt after wow at D3+) · Post: double down on whatever format breaks; Pinterest/Reels/Shorts cross-post; authentic Reddit presence; SEO/AEO content ("369 method," "how to write affirmations").
**Pivot signals (from research):** no TikTok >100K views in ~40 posts → change hook, not budget · D7 <15–20% free → fix loop before growth · install→paid <1% after tuning → rework paywall/pricing · repetition complaints in reviews → generation quality emergency.
