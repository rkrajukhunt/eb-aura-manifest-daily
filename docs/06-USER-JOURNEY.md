# 06 — USER JOURNEY

## Session 1 (minute-by-minute target: install → wow → trial in ≤7 min)

1. **0:00 Splash.** Breathing orb, "Create the life you desire." One line of pricing honesty: "Free to begin. Premium from $X — you'll see everything before you decide." Continue (Terms/Privacy).
2. **0:20 The Conversation** (07-ONBOARDING). 9–11 screens, ~4–5 min. Reflection beats prove listening. Reminder time captured here.
3. **5:00 Generating.** "Thank you for sharing, {name}. Give me a moment… I'm writing you something." Orb breathes; lines of anticipation copy; 15–40s real generation masked as ritual.
4. **5:30 THE LETTER** (08-WOW-MOMENT). 60–90s audio, karaoke text, no controls.
5. **7:00 Paywall** (15). "Your future self has more to tell you." Annual pre-selected + weekly; dismiss once → free tier.
6. **Post-choice:** land on Home with Today's Moment ready + tomorrow's title forming + affirmation waiting. Notification permission primed here (after value, not before).

## Days 1–7 (establish the ritual)

- **D1 evening / D2 morning:** arrival notification in companion voice ("{name} — this morning's is about the studio."). One-tap → player. After listening: today's affirmation card → optional save/share → gratitude line ("One thing from today?"). Done-state: "That's enough for today." Countdown to tomorrow.
- **D3:** first _word echo_ lands (moment reuses an exact phrase from onboarding). First share prompt appears only after a save (never before).
- **D5:** Manifest Anything introduced ("Want a moment about something specific? Type it."). Weekly limit framed as specialness.
- **D7:** **Milestone letter** — short new future-self letter acknowledging the week and quoting a gratitude entry. Streak-lite recap (dots, no shame). Gentle annual upsell for weekly users.

## Day 8–30 (deepen)

- Refine loop teaches memory; playlists/collections accumulate; favorites library grows (investment).
- ~D21: first **callback** ("Three weeks ago you said you were scared to start. Listen to how far the story has come.").
- **D30:** monthly Becoming review — milestone letter + "what's changed in your words" + anniversary echo of the original struggle. New goal thread offered.

## Missed-day journey (critical path — Principle 5)

Any gap: no mention of absence. Return home state: "There you are. Today's moment kept." Dots simply resume. Notifications never escalate; after 3 unopened arrivals, frequency automatically softens (respect > re-engagement).

## Returning subscriber steady-state (the daily 3-minute loop)

Open → greeted by name + date → play today's moment (occasionally containing a remembered detail — the variable reward) → affirmation → gratitude line → done-state. Weekly: one Manifest Anything credit. Monthly: milestone letter.

## Free-tier journey

Daily: 1 moment + 1 affirmation + gratitude. Locked: on-demand, refine, favorites export, collections. Every locked touch shows a calm sheet (no full-screen interrupt) with honest pricing. The free tier is a _daily reminder of the magic_ — good enough to love, small enough to upgrade.

## Churn-risk / win-back

Subscription lapse: no guilt. One warm note at lapse +3 days: a _free_ mini-moment referencing their dream + simple resubscribe. Nothing further. (EXPERIMENT: win-back offer vs plain note.)

## Journey KPIs (targets, first 90 days — from Aura plan)

Onboarding completion >70% · Wow→trial >35% · Trial→paid >40% · D7/D30 paid retention 60%/35% · Affirmation shares/WAU >8% · Qualitative "it knows me" mentions in reviews.
