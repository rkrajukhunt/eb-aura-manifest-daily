# 02 — TARGET AUDIENCE

## Who (RESEARCH FINDING)

- **~70–80% women, core 22–45** (Gen Z + Millennial), meaningful 35–44 secondary. Matches Finch (75% women 25–35), "I Am," and Stella/Aya's clearly female-skewed design.
- **Markets:** US first (≈4x global LTV per download), then UK / CA / AU. English-only at V1; copy architecture ready for localization.
- Highest willingness to pay: US women 25–44 already spending on wellness/self-development.

## Primary personas (synthesized from research + Aura plan)

### The Quiet Dreamer (PRIMARY)

25–40. Has a dream (own business, new city, calmer life) stuck in her Notes app; scrolls manifestation TikTok/IG. Emotional state on open: hope mixed with anxiety and a need for control. Wants: to _hear her dream said out loud, in her name_, and a gentle daily ritual. Pays for: feeling known. Maps to research persona "Manifesting Maya" (24, marketing coordinator, generic affirmations don't resonate).

### The Self-Improver

Already journals/meditates; uses Calm/Headspace. Wants a more personal, identity-focused practice than generic meditations. Values the technique education layer (369, scripting, phrasing science) — she wants to _understand_ the practice, not just consume it. Maps to "Seeker Sam" (structured manifestation, high WTP) and partially "Grateful Grace" (gratitude as stress regulation; falls off when journaling feels like a chore — our one-line entry + AI prompt fixes that).

### The Manifestation Believer

Active in LOA communities; has tried 3–4 manifestation apps and been burned by paywall-first quote decks. Deeply skeptical of billing tricks (see Stella reviews). Wants: real personalization, fair pricing, affirmations that sound like her life. She is the hardest to win and the loudest advocate when won.

### Anti-persona notes ("Anxious Alex" pattern)

Anxious/ADHD users bounce off shame-based streaks and dense interfaces. We don't target them explicitly at V1, but Principle 5 (no guilt) and the tiny daily ask mean we retain them for free.

## Emotional states at open (drives all copy + UX decisions)

Anxiety (self-soothing) · hope (things can change) · desire for control (life feels chaotic) · longing to feel seen. Design consequence: calm palette, warm second-person copy, never clinical, never preachy, no red/neon, no celebratory confetti on low moments.

## Cultural fluency (marketing + optional in-product flavor)

"Lucky girl syndrome," "delulu is the solulu," 369 method, scripting, "living in the end," "romanticize your life," "that girl," "main character energy," manifest a specific person (SP). Use _authentically in marketing_; inside the product keep the companion's voice timeless and warm (trend slang dates fast — RESEARCH CAVEAT). Techniques (369, scripting) appear in-product as _practices_, not slang.

## ASSUMPTIONS to validate

- The Quiet Dreamer converts on the Future-Self Letter specifically (vs generic personalized affirmations). → Measured by wow→trial rate (target >35%).
- Technique education increases perceived credibility and D7 retention rather than adding friction. → A/B in V1.1.
- 22–45 skew holds for an audio-letter product (Stella/Aya screenshots suggest yes).
