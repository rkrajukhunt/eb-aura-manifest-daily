# 00 — PROJECT VISION

> Working name: **Aura** (from project folder; final brand TBD — see 20-OPEN-QUESTIONS).

## One-line pitch

An AI companion that turns your dream life into personalized audio moments, daily affirmations, and gratitude — and gets to know you better every day.

## The core feeling we sell

**"This app knows me."**
Not "this app has good content." Not "this app has AI." The user should feel _seen_: their name, their city, their people, their exact words, reflected back in moments that could not have been made for anyone else.

## What we are building (V1 scope)

A premium, iOS-first manifestation companion with four pillars:

1. **Moments** — personalized first-person audio scenes from the user's future life (the Stella/Aya core mechanic, done with deeper memory).
2. **Affirmations** — AI-generated from the user's own goals and words, created through a short guided-question flow, framed with real techniques (present-tense phrasing, identity statements, 369, scripting).
3. **Gratitude** — a one-line daily entry with AI-personalized prompts; entries feed the memory and reappear inside future moments. _(PRODUCT DECISION: promoted from Phase 2 into a minimal V1 form — see 05-MVP-SCOPE.)_
4. **Living Memory** — the moat. A persistent, transparent, user-editable memory of the user's life that makes every generation more personal over time.

## Why now (RESEARCH FINDING)

- Spiritual-wellness apps ≈ $2.2–2.9B (2024–26), ~14.6% CAGR; manifestation is the fastest-growing, least-consolidated niche.
- Category leader "I Am" (>20M downloads) wins on a static library — its top complaints (repetitive, generic, aggressive paywall) are exactly what AI personalization fixes.
- The new AI wave (Stella $6.99/wk, Aya $9.99/wk) proves users pay weekly for AI future-self audio — but both personalize the _content_, not the _relationship_. Neither remembers. Neither teaches. Both bury pricing behind a long quiz (Stella's 1-star reviews: "bait and switch," "charged me $49 as soon as I downloaded").

## What this app is NOT

- Not a generic affirmation library (everything is generated for one person).
- Not a chatbot (the companion speaks in moments, letters, and short reflections — never an open chat window in V1).
- Not a content feed (one moment + one affirmation + one gratitude line a day is the whole ask).
- Not a feature-heavy wellness dashboard (four tabs, calm screens, generous whitespace).
- Not a web app in a wrapper (iOS-native feel: haptics, sheets, Dynamic Type, 60fps).

## North-star review we're building toward

_"I answered a few questions and it wrote me a letter from my future self — with my name, my city, my mom in it — and I cried. And a month later it remembered something I'd forgotten I told it."_
