# 16 — RETENTION & HABIT LOOPS

## The Hook, mapped honestly

- **Trigger:** internal (morning anxiety, hope) + external (arrival notification in companion voice at chosen time — set during onboarding, Calm's 3x lever).
- **Action:** one tap → today's moment plays (<300ms start; B=MAP-tiny).
- **Variable reward:** genuinely new content daily; occasionally a remembered detail (≤1/week — the scarce magic); rewards of _meaning_, never slot-machine mechanics.
- **Investment:** gratitude line, refine feedback, saved favorites, added people, created affirmations — each visibly deepens memory → tomorrow is better → switching cost compounds.

## The daily ritual (the whole ask, <3 min)

Moment → affirmation → gratitude line → done-state ("That's enough for today") + countdown to tomorrow. Completion is _closed_, not extended — no infinite feed, no "one more." The countdown converts absence into anticipation.

## Anticipation engine

"Coming for you": 2–3 forming moment titles with arrival dates (Aya's best mechanic, simplified). Tomorrow is always slightly visible, never fully given.

## Streak-lite (shame-free by design)

Weekday dots for gratitude/practice. NO break-state exists in the design system — dots simply resume. No 🔥, no loss animations, no repair purchases. (Finch retention lesson; protects the anxious core audience.) Progress framing is identity-based: dots are "evidence of becoming," surfaced in milestone letters, not scoreboard.

## Milestone system (identity checkpoints)

- **D7 letter:** acknowledges the week, quotes one gratitude entry, first "look who's showing up."
- **D30 Becoming review:** letter + "how your words have changed" + anniversary echo of the original struggle + optional new goal thread. (V1.1: automated.)
- **D100 letter:** transformation narration; natural share/testimonial moment (UGC engine — invited, never required).
- Real-life milestones (user marks something manifested — V2 board): celebration moment generated in their honor.

## Callback cadence (from 10)

Word echoes daily (baseline) · remembered detail ≤1/wk · explicit callback ~D21 then monthly · anniversary echo monthly · never >1 explicit callback/day · sensitive items never in notifications.

## Notification policy (retention ≠ nagging)

Arrival note at chosen time (the core loop) · optional affirmation nudge with Aya-grade frequency control (quiet / once a day / your hours) · auto-soften after 3 ignored arrivals · zero guilt vocabulary · every notification passes: "would a caring friend send this?"

## Missed days & lapse

Absence never named; return greeted warmly ("There you are. Today's moment kept."). Subscription lapse: data and Letter kept; one warm win-back note at +3 days; silence after. Respect is the long-game retention strategy in a category built on burned trust.

## Retention metrics & alarms (targets from 06; benchmarks from research)

D1 open-after-notification >60% · D7 paid retention 60% (category median D7 ~25–30% is the _free_ bar) · D30 paid 35% · ritual completion (all 3 beats) >40% of DAU · callback-containing moments: favorite-rate uplift (validates the moat) · pivot signal: repetition/generic complaints appearing in reviews despite personalization = generation quality failure, fix before growth.
