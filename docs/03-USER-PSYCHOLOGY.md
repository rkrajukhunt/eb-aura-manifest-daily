# 03 — USER PSYCHOLOGY

Format per item: **INSIGHT → WHY IT MATTERS → HOW WE APPLY IT → FEATURE/UX IMPLICATION.**
Sources: Steele (self-affirmation theory), Clear (identity-based habits), Eyal (Hook Model), Fogg (B=MAP), Oettingen (fantasy caveat), competitor review mining incl. the Stella App Store screenshot.

## 1. Why users download

**INSIGHT:** Users arrive in anxiety/hope/loss-of-control, seeking agency and a secular spiritual practice (40–43% of women 18–29 now religiously unaffiliated yet ~77–80% self-describe as spiritual).
**WHY:** The download is an emotional act, not a utility purchase.
**APPLY:** First screens promise a feeling ("Create the life you desire" → "Be known"), not features.
**IMPLICATION:** Splash + onboarding copy sells the outcome; zero feature tours.

## 2. Emotional state at open

**INSIGHT:** Charged states — morning anxiety, evening rumination, hope spikes after TikTok content.
**WHY:** A clinical or busy UI at that moment repels; warmth retains.
**APPLY:** Calm gradients, slow breathing orb, soft haptics, short sentences.
**IMPLICATION:** No dense dashboards; the home screen greets by name and offers exactly one thing.

## 3. What makes them complete onboarding

**INSIGHT:** People finish flows that feel like a _conversation leading somewhere_, and abandon quizzes that feel like data harvesting for a paywall (Stella 1-star: "Why make me answer allll those questions & then hit us with the 7 day free trial").
**WHY:** Perceived reciprocity: each answer must visibly buy personalization.
**APPLY:** One question per screen; companion reflects after answers ("John's in. Your circle is forming."); price transparency up front so effort never feels baited.
**IMPLICATION:** Onboarding = 9–11 screens with reflection beats between; edit-guard so nobody restarts; progress implied, not a "step 4/12" meter.

## 4. What makes personalization feel real vs fake

**INSIGHT:** Verbatim specificity — exact name, exact city, exact people, exact phrases — feels real. Paraphrased or category-level personalization ("your career goals") feels like mail merge.
**WHY:** The brain detects template smell instantly; one generic line breaks the spell.
**APPLY:** Generation prompts must inject literal user strings; never normalize "my little cottage in Cornwall" into "your dream home."
**IMPLICATION:** Memory stores exact phrases (10-LIVING-MEMORY); QA rule: every moment must contain ≥3 literal user-specific tokens.

## 5. What creates the first WOW

**INSIGHT:** The payoff moment where everything they typed comes back _transformed_ — a voice speaking their future life to them by name (Stella 5-star: "It gives me goosebumps it's so real I can see it all unfold").
**WHY:** It converts a form into a relationship in 90 seconds.
**APPLY:** The Future-Self Letter (08-WOW-MOMENT), staged with anticipation, audio-first, no controls.
**IMPLICATION:** Everything in onboarding exists to set it up; wow→trial is a north-star metric.

## 6. What creates trust

**INSIGHT:** Trust = confidentiality framing + visible control + honest billing. Distrust = surprise charges (Stella: "they charged me $49 as soon as I downloaded"), fake urgency, hidden weekly pricing.
**WHY:** This audience is pre-burned; trust is the differentiator, not a hygiene factor.
**APPLY:** "Nothing you share leaves this room" + Never-Include list + memory transparency screen + price shown before the quiz.
**IMPLICATION:** Privacy/memory controls are V1 features (10, 18); paywall follows anti-resentment rules (15).

## 7. What makes users return tomorrow

**INSIGHT:** Anticipation of something made _for them_ arriving ("Coming for you," 24h countdown) + a reminder set during onboarding (Calm: reminder-in-onboarding = 3x retention).
**WHY:** Return must be pulled by curiosity, not pushed by guilt.
**APPLY:** Tomorrow's moment title visible but "forming"; arrival notification in companion voice at user-chosen time.
**IMPLICATION:** Reminder-time question is an onboarding screen; notifications sound like a person, never marketing.

## 8. What creates a daily habit

**INSIGHT:** B=MAP — tiny action at low motivation. One tap to listen; one line of gratitude.
**WHY:** Wellness apps die in week 1 (>50% abandon; median D15 ~3.9%).
**APPLY:** The whole daily ritual is <3 minutes; the app celebrates completion and closes the loop ("that's enough for today").
**IMPLICATION:** No infinite content; countdown chip communicates "done for today" as a feature.

## 9. What creates attachment to the companion

**INSIGHT:** Parasocial bonds form with a consistent character voice that remembers and cares (Finch: "a small companion glad to see you back"; Aya's named character outperforms Stella's tool-voice emotionally).
**WHY:** Attachment, not content volume, is the retention engine.
**APPLY:** Aura speaks as "I," has a consistent literary-warm voice, references shared history.
**IMPLICATION:** Companion voice guide (14) governs every string; callbacks engine (10) is core.

## 10. What makes users subscribe

**INSIGHT:** Subscription follows a felt "it knows me" moment + desire for the ongoing relationship ("Your future self has more to tell you").
**WHY:** Feature checklists don't convert this audience; feelings do.
**APPLY:** Paywall immediately after the Letter, copy mirroring the wow, showing what continues (daily moments, memory deepening).
**IMPLICATION:** Paywall spec in 15; ~89% of conversions happen session 1 — the first paywall is the highest-leverage screen after the Letter itself.

## 11. What causes paywall resentment

**INSIGHT:** Long effort → surprise price; weekly pricing perceived as predatory ($10/wk = "$40 a month US #, about $65-70 Canadian!! Be honest!"); charges despite trial promises; ads for paying users.
**WHY:** Resentment produces 1-star reviews that poison ASO (rating volume is a top ranking factor).
**APPLY:** Price disclosed pre-quiz; monthly-equivalent shown next to weekly; trial terms restated at confirmation; generous cancel path.
**IMPLICATION:** Anti-resentment checklist in 15 is release-blocking.

## 12. What makes users share organically

**INSIGHT:** People share artifacts that flatter their identity: beautiful affirmation cards, "this app wrote me a letter and I cried" stories.
**WHY:** Organic TikTok/Pinterest is the only viable growth engine for a bootstrapped founder.
**APPLY:** Share-as-image cards (clean, branded, aesthetic) on affirmations; screen-recordable wow.
**IMPLICATION:** Card renderer is V1; share event is a tracked activation metric (17).

## 13. Variable rewards, ethically

**INSIGHT:** Fresh, surprising, personally-relevant content is the reward ("rewards of the self") — the antidote to "I Am's" repetition complaint.
**WHY:** Variability sustains dopamine without manipulation _if_ the variance is in meaning, not in slot-machine mechanics.
**APPLY:** Each day's moment/affirmation is genuinely new and occasionally includes a remembered detail (the strongest variable reward we have). No loot boxes, no random unlock timers.
**IMPLICATION:** "Remembered detail" injection is a scheduled memory behavior (10); never gamble-style UI.

## 14. Identity-based habits

**INSIGHT:** "Every action is a vote for the person you wish to become" (Clear). Affirmations ARE identity statements.
**WHY:** Identity framing ("you're becoming her") outlasts outcome framing ("get things").
**APPLY:** Progress copy frames evidence of becoming; milestone letters (D7/D30/D100) narrate identity change from entries; affirmation generator defaults to "I am…" identity phrasing.
**IMPLICATION:** Milestones in 16; affirmation phrasing rules in 09 + 14.

## 15. Investment increases value

**INSIGHT:** Every entry (gratitude line, added person, refined moment, saved favorite) makes the product more valuable and harder to leave (Hook Model: Investment).
**WHY:** Investment is the compounding moat competitors can't copy.
**APPLY:** All inputs visibly feed memory; "Refine" feedback teaches the generator; favorites become a personal library.
**IMPLICATION:** Every input surface writes to memory (10); show the link ("Aura will remember this").

## 16. Memory and callbacks → "it remembers me"

**INSIGHT:** The single strongest trigger is a small detail mentioned once and forgotten, resurfacing naturally weeks later.
**WHY:** It's unfakeable proof of relationship; neither competitor does it.
**APPLY:** Callback engine schedules echoes: 3-week callbacks, anniversary echoes ("A month ago today you told me about…"), people-name reuse, progress noticing.
**IMPLICATION:** Full spec in 10; micro-wow cadence rules prevent overuse (uncanny if too frequent).

## 17. Avoiding guilt and shame on missed days

**INSIGHT:** Punitive streaks churn exactly the anxious users this category serves; Finch's shame-free model retains them.
**WHY:** Guilt converts a refuge into a chore.
**APPLY:** Streaks shown as gentle weekday dots with no break-state; return copy = warmth ("There you are. Today's moment kept.") — never "you missed 3 days."
**IMPLICATION:** No streak-loss states exist in the design system; notification copy bank contains zero guilt variants (14).

## Duty-of-care note (RESEARCH CAVEAT)

Positive fantasizing alone can reduce motivation (Oettingen); manifestation framing can shade into denial for struggling users. Rules: moments depict agency ("you did the work and here you are"); struggle references are gentle and user-authored; if a user discloses crisis-level content in free text, the app responds with warmth + signposts help and never generates "manifest it away" content (18-PRIVACY-SAFETY).
