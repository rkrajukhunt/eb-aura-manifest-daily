# 04 — COMPETITIVE INSIGHTS

## Direct competitors (deep teardown available — Stella_Aya_Extraction)

### Stella — "Manifest Anything" ($6.99/wk, 7-day trial)

- **Copy:** 4-tab nav (Home · Affirmations · Gratitude · Profile); conversational onboarding (self-description, work feeling, values, dream home, dream city, people, struggle); Today's Moment with 24h refresh countdown; long-prose story player with iridescent orb, 7 selectable TTS voices, ambient sound mixer (Rain/Café/City/Beach/Wind), Read-Instead synced text; Modify Story regeneration; affirmation swipe cards + share-as-image + photo overlay; gratitude streak (weekday dots); Manifestations wall; rich profile incl. **Never Include** exclusion tags and "Anything Stella should know?" free text; delete-account with full wipe.
- **Fix (from the App Store reviews screenshot, 4.6★/381):** Billing bait-and-switch ("tried the free first week they charged me $49 as soon as I downloaded"); price revealed only after long quiz ("answer allll those questions & then hit us with… $10/week!!! Greedy & misleading"); "$40 a month US #… Be honest!"; wow gated _behind_ the paywall; fake-urgency paywall letter ("overwhelming demand"). Also: no memory, no callbacks, no education.
- **What their 5-stars prove:** the core mechanic lands hard — "I created a story… the next day it happened!", "It gives me goosebumps it's so real I can see it all unfold."

### Aya — "Manifest Your Dream Self" ($9.99/wk, no trial)

- **Copy:** Named character speaking in first person; poetic literary copy; "forming" drip-scheduled moments with future dates (long anticipation loop); onboarding edit-guard + encouraging reflections ("John's in too. Your circle's forming."); granular notification controls (frequency, wake/wind-down windows); home/lock-screen widget (6 themes); read-aloud 3× affirmation practice with speech recognition; contrast paywall (Where you are / Where you're going) + social proof.
- **Fix:** Second "quieter price just for you" discount paywall (manipulation smell); single voice; no memory beyond profile; $9.99/wk price ceiling; no gratitude, no share cards.

## Category leader & references (RESEARCH FINDING)

- **"I Am" (Monkey Taps):** >20M downloads, ~$35.99/yr, #1 ASO ("affirmations" keyword ≈554K downloads/yr). Weaknesses = our wedge: repetitive after two days, generic, aggressive paywall post-quiz, removed custom affirmations. Lesson: keyword-in-title + rating volume wins search; card aesthetic drives shares.
- **Finch:** $30–40M ARR bootstrapped, 75% women 25–35. Lesson: shame-free companionship + genuinely good free tier converts better than locked doors.
- **Calm:** reminder-setup in onboarding = 3x retention (adoption <1% → 40% when moved into onboarding). Sell the outcome, not the feature.
- **Headspace:** warmth/illustration de-stigmatizes; cut 38% onboarding drop-off by simplifying steps.
- **To Be Magnetic:** $324/yr × ~18K members proves WTP for _structured, credible_ manifestation depth → validates our technique-education layer.
- **Innertune:** own-voice recording thesis + 326M organic views → validates share-card + screen-recordable-feature growth; own-voice is a V2 candidate.

## The five exploitable gaps (synthesis)

1. **No one remembers.** Stella/Aya personalize content, not the relationship. → Living Memory (10).
2. **No one is honest about price.** → Radical pricing transparency (15). Trust as positioning.
3. **No one teaches.** Users stitch techniques from TikTok/Pinterest. → Guided affirmation flow + technique cards (09).
4. **Wow is wasted.** Stella spends it after payment. → Wow before paywall (08).
5. **Gratitude is disconnected.** Stella's gratitude is a dead-end journal. → Memory-fed gratitude that resurfaces inside moments (09).
